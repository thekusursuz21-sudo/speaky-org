Online Language Exchange & Conversation Practice

Learn how to structure useful conversations where two people help each other practice different languages.

What Is Language Exchange?

A language exchange pairs people who want to practice languages they can help each other with. Instead of a formal lesson, participants use conversation to improve confidence, listening and everyday vocabulary.

Make Practice Fair

Agree on expectations before starting. You might spend half the conversation in each language or alternate languages on different days. Correct every mistake only if your partner wants that level of feedback.

Choose a Language

Explore English, Spanish, German, French, Italian or Turkish conversation resources.

Protect Your Privacy

Language practice does not require sharing passwords, financial information or identity documents. Review our online chat safety guide and scam awareness guide.
