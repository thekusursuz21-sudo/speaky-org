Turkish Chat Rooms

Meet Turkish speakers online, discover conversations about Turkey and connect with people who share an interest in Turkish culture and everyday life.

Chat With Turkish Speakers Online

Turkish chat communities bring together people from Istanbul, Ankara, Izmir, Antalya and cities across Turkey, as well as Turkish speakers living throughout Europe and the rest of the world. They can be a simple way to meet people, talk about shared interests and discover different perspectives.

Looking for a Turkish-Language Community?

Speaky.org is focused on helping visitors discover chat communities in English. If you prefer a dedicated Turkish-language experience, Çatlak.org Turkish chat offers a community built specifically for Turkish-speaking users.

That makes it useful for people who want to continue conversations in Turkish rather than relying on an international English-language community.

Popular Topics in Turkish Chat

Conversations can range from music, football and films to travel, food, relationships, local life and meeting new friends. A friendly introduction and a genuine interest in the conversation are usually the easiest places to start.

Stay Safe When Meeting People Online

Do not share passwords, financial details, authentication codes or other sensitive information with people you have just met. Read the Speaky.org online chat safety guide for practical advice.
