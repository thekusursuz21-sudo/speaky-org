German Chat & Conversation Resources

Discover practical resources for conversations with German speakers, friendship and language practice.

Meet German Speakers Online

German-speaking communities include people in Germany, Austria, Switzerland and international communities around the world. Conversation gives learners and travelers a chance to understand everyday language beyond textbooks.

Conversation Ideas

Try hobbies, travel, technology, music, food or daily life. Specific questions usually produce better conversations than a generic hello.

Practice Languages Fairly

For language practice, alternate languages or divide conversation time so both participants receive useful practice. See our language exchange guide.

Explore Safely

Find broader communities through European chat and international chat. Review our online safety advice before sharing personal information.
