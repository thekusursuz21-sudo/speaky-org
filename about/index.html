About Speaky.org

Speaky.org is being built around a simple idea: make it easier for people to find conversations and meet new people online.

Conversation Without the Complication

We want Speaky.org to feel straightforward from the moment you arrive. Choose a community, discover people with similar interests and start talking without navigating an unnecessarily complicated experience.

A Global Community

Our growing collection of topic and location pages is designed to help people find conversations that suit them, from friendship and singles chat to communities for Ireland, the UK, the USA and beyond.

Safety and Respect

Healthy communities depend on respectful behaviour. Speaky.org promotes privacy awareness, sensible online safety and conversations that respect other people.
