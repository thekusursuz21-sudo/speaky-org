Meet People Across Canada

Canada's communities span major cities, smaller towns and two widely used official languages. Online conversations can connect people around local life, travel, sport, entertainment and cultural interests.

Conversation Topics

Ask about cities, outdoor activities, food, music, hockey, travel or everyday routines. Avoid treating a large and diverse country as if everyone has the same experience.
