Good Conversations Cross Borders

You do not need to know everything about another country to start talking. Asking respectful questions about food, travel, music, sport or everyday life is often enough to begin a friendly conversation.

Protect Your Privacy

Meeting people internationally should not mean giving strangers sensitive information. Keep passwords, financial information and private identifying details to yourself, and review our chat safety guide.
