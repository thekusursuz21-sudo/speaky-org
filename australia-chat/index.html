Australia Chat & Online Communities

Explore resources for conversations with people across Australia, from shared interests to travel and friendship.

Meet People Across Australia

Online communities can connect people across Australia's widely separated cities and regions. Conversations often grow naturally from shared interests rather than location alone.

What Can You Talk About?

Travel, beaches, sport, music, films, food, work and local events are useful starting points. Ask about someone's own experience instead of relying on familiar stereotypes.
