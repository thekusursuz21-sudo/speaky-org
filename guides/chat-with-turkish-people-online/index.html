Chat With Turkish People Online

A useful conversation starts with curiosity, respect and something more interesting than a one-word hello.

Where to begin

People looking to chat with Turkish people online may be interested in friendship, language practice, travel, culture or simply meeting someone new. Turkish speakers live not only in Turkey but across Germany, the Netherlands, the UK, Ireland and many other countries, so there is no single Turkish online experience.

Start with context. Instead of sending only “hi,” mention why you joined the conversation. You might ask about a city you want to visit, a Turkish dish you have tried, a football match, a film or a piece of music. Questions that leave room for an opinion tend to produce better replies.

Good conversation topics

Food, travel, music, sport, films, technology and everyday life are easy starting points. Turkey is geographically and culturally diverse, so avoid assuming that someone from Istanbul, Izmir, Ankara, Antalya or a smaller town has the same background or views as everyone else.

If you are learning Turkish, say so. Most people are more patient with mistakes when they know you are practicing. Our guide to Turkish conversation starters gives you simple ideas for opening a chat naturally.

Friendship rather than interrogation

A conversation should move both ways. Share a little about yourself rather than firing question after question. Keep personal questions appropriate to how well you know the other person, and do not pressure anyone to move immediately to another platform.

Understand the setting

Public rooms, interest communities and one-to-one conversations have different expectations. Read the room before joining in. For more context, see our guide to Turkish online chat culture and the main Turkish chat hub.

Stay safe

Friendly conversation does not require passwords, verification codes, bank details or copies of identity documents. Be cautious when a new contact quickly asks for money, presents an emergency or pressures you to keep a conversation secret. Speaky's online chat safety guide explains the basics.
