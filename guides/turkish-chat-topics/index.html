Best Turkish Chat Topics

Better topics turn a greeting into a conversation. Use these ideas as starting points rather than a script.

Food

Ask which regional dish someone would recommend, how they prefer Turkish coffee, or what food visitors misunderstand. Food works well because answers often connect to family, city and personal memories.

Travel and cities

Instead of asking only whether someone likes Istanbul, ask what they would show a friend visiting their city for one day. Turkey's regional variety creates plenty of room for recommendations and comparisons.

Music, films and television

Ask what someone is currently watching or listening to. Recommendations give you an easy follow-up topic later and can introduce you to Turkish-language media.

Football and sport

Football can generate energetic conversation, but rivalries can be intense. Ask what someone follows before assuming their team. Sport beyond football can also be a useful topic.

Language

If you are learning Turkish, ask about an expression you heard, pronunciation or the difference between formal and casual language. See our Turkish online slang guide for context.

Everyday life

Work, study, hobbies, weekend plans, technology and local routines often produce the most natural friendships because they focus on the person rather than their nationality.

Follow the answer

The best topic is often hidden inside the other person's previous message. Ask a follow-up, share your own experience and allow the conversation to change direction.
