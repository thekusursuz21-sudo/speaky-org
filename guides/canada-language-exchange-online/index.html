Canada Language Exchange Online

Use conversations connected with Canada to practise English or French while keeping the exchange useful for both people.

Choose the Language Goal

Canada has English- and French-speaking communities, but never assume which language another person prefers. Ask first and agree on the purpose of the exchange.

Balance Practice

If both participants are learners, alternate languages or divide practice time so each person benefits.

Use Real Conversation

Regional life, travel, food, entertainment and everyday routines provide useful vocabulary in context. See Canada conversation topics.
