UK Conversation Topics for Online Chat

Use open questions to discover individual experiences across England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland.

Start With Interests

Music, television, gaming, food, work and hobbies give both people room to contribute without relying on regional stereotypes.

Sport Without Assumptions

Football may be popular, but ask what the other person follows before building a conversation around it. Rugby, cricket and many other interests may be more relevant.

Regional Travel

Questions about cities, countryside, coastlines and places someone recommends can reveal different experiences across the UK.
