USA Conversation Topics for Online Chat

The United States contains many regional experiences, so questions work better than assumptions.

Everyday Interests

Work, school, entertainment, food, hobbies and weekend plans are flexible subjects that work across different states and age groups.

Regional Differences

Ask what life is like where someone lives or what visitors misunderstand about their area. This can reveal differences between large cities, suburbs, smaller towns and regions.

Travel and Time Zones

Travel recommendations can lead to conversations about climate, distances and local attractions. Remember that online conversations across the USA may span several time zones.
