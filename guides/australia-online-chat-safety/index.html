Australia Online Chat Safety

Protect your privacy while meeting and talking with people online.

Keep Sensitive Details Private

Do not share passwords, banking information, verification codes or your home address with someone simply because a conversation feels friendly.

Be Careful With Money Requests

Unexpected emergencies, investment opportunities and requests to transfer money should be treated cautiously, especially when trust has been built only online.

If You Meet Offline

Choose a public location, arrange independent transport and tell someone you trust about the meeting.
