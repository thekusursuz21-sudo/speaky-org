European Online Chat Guide

Better conversations across European communities begin with curiosity about individual experiences rather than assumptions.

Europe is not one conversation culture

Communication styles, languages and everyday experiences vary widely between countries and regions. Ask rather than assume how someone prefers to communicate.

Use cross-cultural topics carefully

Travel, food, music, sport and language are accessible starting points. When politics, identity or history comes up, listen to how the other person describes their own perspective.

Connect location and language

European conversations often cross language boundaries. Use the Languages hub and Language Exchange when language practice is part of the conversation.

Explore the cluster

Visit European Chat, browse individual countries in Locations, and read international chat etiquette.
