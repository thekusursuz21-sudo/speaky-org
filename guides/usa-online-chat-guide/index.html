USA Online Chat Guide

Conversation ideas for connecting around life, interests and places across the United States.

Think regionally

The United States contains very different climates, cities, cultures and routines. Questions about a person's own region or interests are usually more useful than broad assumptions about American life.

Use shared interests

Sports, music, films, technology, food, travel and hobbies can all create easy openings. Follow the topics the other person actually enjoys.

Keep personal information private

A friendly conversation does not require an exact address, financial information or private documents. Build trust gradually and use platform safety features when necessary.
