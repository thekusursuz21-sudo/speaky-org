Australia Conversation Topics for Online Chat

Build conversations around real interests rather than assumptions about Australian life.

Everyday Interests

Work, study, music, sport, food, gaming and weekend plans create easy openings and give the other person several directions to take the conversation.

Travel and Distance

Australia's geography makes travel an interesting subject. Ask about places someone recommends, trips they have taken or differences between regions.

Australian English

If language learning is your goal, ask about unfamiliar expressions in context instead of collecting slang lists. Continue with English Chat.
