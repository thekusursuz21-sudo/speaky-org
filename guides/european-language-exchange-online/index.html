European Language Exchange Online

Multilingual conversations can connect language practice with everyday life across Europe.

Ask Which Language Someone Wants to Use

Do not assume language from nationality or location. Many people use several languages, and the best exchange starts with a clear shared goal.

Balance Both Learners

Alternate languages by time or topic when both people are practising. Keep corrections selective so conversation still feels natural.

Use Cross-Cultural Topics

Travel, food, music, local customs and everyday routines provide useful context. See European conversation topics.
