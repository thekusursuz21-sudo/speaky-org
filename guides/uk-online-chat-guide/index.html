UK Online Chat Guide

Use shared interests and regional curiosity to create better UK-focused conversations online.

Choose a real topic

Music, football, films, travel, food, work and local events provide easy openings. Asking a specific question usually gives someone more to respond to than a generic greeting.

Remember the UK is diverse

England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland contain many distinct communities and identities. Avoid treating one city, accent or experience as representative of everyone.

Use conversation to learn

If a phrase, custom or regional reference is unfamiliar, ask about it. Curiosity can turn a small detail into a natural conversation.
