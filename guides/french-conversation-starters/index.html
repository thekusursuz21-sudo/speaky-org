French Conversation Starters for Online Chat

Useful topics and follow-up habits for turning a simple bonjour into a comfortable conversation.

Choose topics with easy follow-ups

Travel, food, films, music and daily routines work well because an answer naturally creates another question. Ask about a favorite local dish, a city worth visiting or what someone likes doing after work.

Write for conversation, not an exam

Clear short messages make it easier for both people to participate. Learners can use familiar French first, then add new expressions gradually instead of trying to write perfect complex sentences immediately.

Learn from real replies

Notice how the other person phrases everyday ideas and ask about unfamiliar expressions. Real exchanges expose you to vocabulary and tone that textbook exercises may not emphasize.
