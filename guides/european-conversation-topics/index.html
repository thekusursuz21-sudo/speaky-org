European Conversation Topics for Online Chat

Europe is multilingual and culturally varied, so curiosity works better than broad assumptions.

Languages and Learning

Ask which languages someone uses, is learning or encounters in daily life. Explore the Languages hub for dedicated English, Spanish, German, French, Italian and Turkish resources.

Travel Across Borders

Favourite cities, train journeys, regional food and places worth revisiting can create conversations without treating Europe as a single culture.

Everyday Differences

Work routines, education, entertainment and local customs can vary between countries and regions. Ask people about their own experience instead of expecting a universal answer.
