UK Online Chat Etiquette

Respectful conversation matters more than trying to imitate a particular British style.

Do Not Treat the UK as One Culture

England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland contain many regional identities. Ask about someone's own experience rather than assuming preferences or background.

Humour Needs Context

Dry humour and teasing can be misunderstood in text. If you do not know someone well, clarity is usually better than forcing jokes or slang.

Respect Boundaries

Do not pressure people for private information, photos or immediate replies. Friendly conversation allows the other person to choose what they share.
