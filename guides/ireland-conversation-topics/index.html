Ireland Conversation Topics for Online Chat

Shared interests are a better starting point than assumptions about where someone lives.

Everyday Life

Work, study, weekend plans and local events are easy subjects that let people describe their own experience rather than speak for an entire country.

Music, Sport and Entertainment

Ask what someone actually follows. Football, rugby, GAA, music, films and live events can all work, but personal interest matters more than stereotypes.

Travel Around Ireland

Places people enjoy, day trips, coastal areas and city visits can lead to useful conversations about Dublin, Cork, Galway, Wexford and other parts of Ireland.
