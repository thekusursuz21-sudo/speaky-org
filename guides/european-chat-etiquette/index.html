European Chat Etiquette

Cross-cultural conversation works best when differences become questions rather than assumptions.

Europe Is Not One Culture

Communication styles, languages and social expectations vary between countries, regions and individuals. Let the other person describe their own experience.

Keep Language Clear

When people use a second language, simple wording can prevent misunderstandings. Explain idioms when needed instead of assuming they are familiar.

Be Careful With Humour and Stereotypes

Jokes often depend on cultural context. Avoid using national stereotypes as conversation shortcuts, especially with someone you have just met.
