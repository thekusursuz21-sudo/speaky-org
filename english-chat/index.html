English Chat & Conversation Practice

Discover useful ways to practice everyday English through real conversations with people from different countries.

Practice English Through Conversation

Online conversation can help learners become more comfortable with everyday vocabulary, questions and natural replies. Start with simple topics such as work, hobbies, travel, food and entertainment rather than trying to make every message perfect.

Meet English Speakers Worldwide

English is used across many international communities. Explore our international chat, UK chat, Ireland chat and USA chat resources for different conversation contexts.

Start Better Conversations

Use open questions and give the other person something easy to respond to. Our guide on how to start a conversation online offers practical examples.

Stay Safe

Keep passwords, verification codes, financial details and private documents out of online conversations. Read our online chat safety guide before using unfamiliar communities.
