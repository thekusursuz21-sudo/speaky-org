Frequently Asked Questions

What is Speaky.org?

Speaky.org is a growing directory of online chat community pages and practical guides for people interested in online conversations.

Do I need to download an app?

No. Speaky.org's current resources are available through modern mobile and desktop browsers.

What communities can I explore?

You can browse friendship, singles, international, Turkish, UK, Ireland, USA and other community-focused pages.

How can I stay safer online?

Keep sensitive information private, be cautious with financial requests and read our dedicated safety and scam-awareness guides.
